How the market works. Start to finish.

The full path for a seller and a buyer, from the first listing to the moment the share changes hands.

Selling

  1. Choose how muchAny part of your share, in steps of 0.5%. Sell 2% of a 10% share and keep the other 8%.
  2. Set your priceYou set the price per 1%. The ticket shows the reference value and where your price sits against it before you list.
  3. Sell now or listSell to the best open offer at once, or list at your price and wait for a buyer.
  4. While it's listedThe listed part is locked so it can't be sold twice. It still earns Yield and still votes. Cancel any time for free.
  5. A buyer takes itAll of it or part of it, down to 0.5%. The rest stays listed at the same price.
  6. SettlementUSDC lands in your balance minus the 2.5% fee. The share leaves your project page. Listings that don't sell expire after 30 days.

Buying

  1. Pick a projectEach car trades on its own. Compare the price per 1% with the reference value and the trade history.
  2. Buy now or make an offerBuy now takes the cheapest listings first. An offer sits in the book at your price until a seller accepts it or you cancel it.
  3. See the full cost firstThe ticket shows which listings fill, the average price and the total. Buyers pay no fee.
  4. PayUSDC leaves your balance and the trade settles in one step. There's no waiting period.
  5. Own itThe share appears in My projects at once, with its ownership percentage.
  6. Votes and YieldYour voting power counts from the next vote. Yield is paid to whoever holds the share on the payout date.
Part or whole?Any part

Sell any amount from 0.5% up to everything you hold, in 0.5% steps. Buyers can also take part of a listing.

Who sets the price?The seller

The seller names the price per 1%. Buyers can also post offers. The book shows both sides, so the spread is visible.

Fair price hintReference value

The car's latest valuation divided by 100. Within 10% of it is marked fair. Beyond 20%, the ticket warns you before you list.

Fees2.5% on the seller

Taken from the proceeds when a trade settles. Listing, cancelling and offers are free. Buyers pay no fee.

Votes during a saleFixed when a vote opens

Voting power is counted when a vote opens. A share sold mid-vote still counts for the seller in that vote. The buyer votes from the next one. This stops anyone from buying a vote.

LimitsMax 50% per owner

No one can buy above half of a car, so no single owner decides a vote alone. Listings last 30 days, and offers last 7.

Example

You hold 10% of Project #1847, a 911 with a reference value of $936 per 1%. You list 2% at $950. A buyer takes 1.5% the next day: you receive $1,389.38 ($1,425 minus the $35.63 fee), and 0.5% stays listed. A vote opened two days ago, so your full 10% still counts in it. The buyer votes with 1.5% from the next vote.
