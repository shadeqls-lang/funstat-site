PHASE 1 In design

Share Market. Sell part, keep the rest.

Every car gets its own market. List any part of your share at your price, or buy into a car that already exists.

  • Sell any partFrom 0.5% up to everything you hold.
  • Price hint on every ticketYour price against the car's latest valuation.
  • Votes can't be bought mid-voteVoting power is fixed when a vote opens.

PHASE 3 Planned

Race Manager. Your car, your team.

Owners run their car like a team. Vote to send it to a race, hire a driver from our pool, set the strategy, and split the prize money by share.

  1. Vote to enterOwners decide which races the car runs.
  2. Hire a driverFrom the Vaultline driver pool.
  3. Set strategyTyres, fuel, how hard to push.
  4. RaceOn the Vaultline Ring, against other projects.
  5. Split the prizePaid out by share, like Yield.

PHASE 4 Planned

Upgrade Spin. Trade up, with the risk on the table.

If owners vote for it, the project's value goes into a Spin for a more expensive car. Every outcome, every chance and the worst case are shown before anyone commits.

Safe
25% step up 1.2×, 50% keep, 25% lose 15%.
Risk
20% double up, 30% keep, 50% lose 40%.
Jackpot
3% jump 5×, once per project. 80% lose 30%.
