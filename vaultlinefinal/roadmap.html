Roadmap questions.

How we plan, what the dates mean and how your vote counts.

How often is this page updated?

Every time a milestone moves, and at least once a month. The date under the title shows the last update.

Are the target dates promises?

No. They're the quarter we're aiming for today. If a phase slips, we move the date here and say why.

How do votes decide the order?

Owners vote on what comes after the phases already in progress. A vote weighs as much as the owner's share, so people with more at stake have more say.

Will new features cost extra?

Using them won't. The Share Market takes a small fee from the seller on each trade, and the exact number is one of the open questions above.

Can I try anything before it's live?

Yes. The Share Market preview and the 3D 911 are up now, and Share Market will open a beta for 50 owners before it goes public.

What happens to ideas I send?

The team reads all of them. Ideas that keep coming up are added to the vote, so owners can push them up the list.
