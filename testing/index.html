Before you join.

Straight answers about shares, votes and risk.

What do I own after a Spin?
A fractional share of one project, and each project holds one real vehicle. Your share also gives you the same percentage of voting power. On this concept page, USDC, projects and cars are simulated.
How is my share decided?
Your contribution divided by the pool size, fixed before the Spin. $5,000 in a $50,000 pool is 10%, whether the Vault lands on $30,000 or $250,000.
What if the Spin lands on a small Vault?
Your percentage stays the same, and the project buys a less expensive car. The odds and the expected Vault are published above, so you can see the range before you contribute.
Does the platform own the cars in advance?
No. Once the Vault is set, the platform sources a matching car through dealers and marketplaces, buys it, and creates the project.
Who decides what happens to the car?
The owners. Each owner votes with their voting power for Sell, Yield, Hold, Upgrade or Recycle, and the option with the most voting power when voting closes is applied.
How does an Upgrade Spin limit risk?
Every mode lists its outcomes, odds and maximum loss before anyone commits. Jackpot Upgrades are limited to one attempt per project.
Can I leave a project early?
Yes. List any part of your share on the project's marketplace. When a buyer takes it, ownership and voting power move to them right away.
Who can take part?
Adults 18 and over, where this kind of product is allowed. Spending limits and cool-off periods can be set from your account.
