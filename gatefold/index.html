Questions

Before you rip

Still unsure? Open a pool and read every ticket inside. Nothing is hidden.

Are these real tickets?

Yes. Every ticket in a pool is bought from the official seller or a verified resale partner before the pool opens, and it goes to your phone like any other ticket.

Can I choose the game or the seat?

Not in a rip, that's the point. You choose the pool. The machine chooses the ticket at the published odds.

What if I can't make it?

Sell it back to us for 90% of its value within 7 days of the pull. After that you can still resell it at face value.

How are the odds set?

Each pool has four tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare and Epic. Their chances add up to 100%, are shown before you pay, and stay fixed for the life of the pool.

Is there a limit?

Five rips a day by default. You can lower it or pause rips any time. 18+ only, and only where prize games like this are allowed.
